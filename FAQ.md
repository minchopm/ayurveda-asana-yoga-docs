# Ayurveda & Asana Yoga — frequently asked questions

## What should I check when using traditional framework?

These are traditional cultural frameworks, not medical diagnoses or established clinical claims.

## What should I check when using nutrition?

A reference entry does not establish an individual's requirements or the composition of every prepared meal.

## What should I check when using movement?

A library cannot assess personal injury risk. Choose suitable practices and follow relevant professional advice.

## Where should I start?

Use the quick-start guide with a small example, verify the saved result, then choose a practical workflow. Avoid beginning with irreplaceable data or an entire production project.

## How do I report a documentation or app problem?

Use the official product support channel for account or purchase issues. A public GitHub issue is suitable for documentation corrections and sanitized reproduction steps. Include the app version, device/OS, expected outcome and actual result; do not attach private data.
