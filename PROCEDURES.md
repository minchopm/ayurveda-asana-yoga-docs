# Ayurveda & Asana Yoga — operating procedures

Use these procedures as task-oriented reference. They describe public capabilities and verification steps, not private implementation. UI labels can vary by version.

## Procedure 1: Review a suggested meal and routine

### Before you begin

Separate traditional context from measured nutrient information.

### Steps

1. Read the traditional framing as a cultural lens.

2. Check actual ingredients, serving quantities and allergens.

3. Inspect nutrient fields on their own terms.

4. Choose a movement practice suited to your ability and read cautions.

5. Adjust the plan to available time and circumstances.

### Expected result

The plan is practical and its traditional and nutritional claims are not conflated.

### If the result differs

Traditional tags are not diagnoses. A generated plan does not establish suitability for a medical condition.

## Procedure 2: Prepare a comfortable practice session

### Before you begin

Choose familiar movements and adequate space.

### Steps

1. Review the sequence and individual cautions before starting.

2. Allow time for transitions and rest.

3. Use a comfortable pace rather than treating completion as a target.

4. Stop for pain, dizziness or distress.

5. Record practical adjustments for the next session.

### Expected result

The routine is adapted to your circumstances rather than followed mechanically.

### If the result differs

The app cannot assess personal injury risk; seek appropriate professional advice when needed.

## Report a repeatable problem

Record app/version, device/OS, selected mode, input conditions and the smallest failing step. Distinguish expected behavior from the observed result. Use synthetic examples and omit credentials or private records.
