# Ayurveda & Asana Yoga — user documentation

Meal and daily-routine planning with traditional Ayurvedic context, nutrient data and a yoga practice library.

## At a glance

| Capability | What the product documents | Boundary to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traditional framework | Six-taste and constitution/seasonal concepts are used to organize routines. | These are traditional cultural frameworks, not medical diagnoses or established clinical claims. |
| Nutrition | USDA-referenced nutrient information accompanies meal planning. | A reference entry does not establish an individual's requirements or the composition of every prepared meal. |
| Movement | Illustrated posture and breath/rest practice library with cues and cautions. | A library cannot assess personal injury risk. Choose suitable practices and follow relevant professional advice. |
