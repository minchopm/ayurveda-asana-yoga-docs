# Ayurveda & Asana Yoga — practical workflows

These are suggested user procedures, not claims that automated tests have been performed. Each workflow has a concrete outcome to inspect.

## Plan a meal with two kinds of information

Read the traditional taste or seasonal framing separately from measured nutrient fields. Check ingredients and portion quantities before using nutrient totals. Neither a traditional label nor an automated suggestion establishes that a food is appropriate for an allergy or medical condition.

## Build a gentle practice sequence

Choose familiar movements and leave time for transitions and rest. Read the plain-language cautions rather than selecting a pose only by its image. Stop if a movement causes pain, dizziness or distress; the app cannot assess those symptoms.

## Review a weekly routine

Compare what you planned with what you actually did. Look for practical issues such as unavailable ingredients or an unrealistic practice duration. Adjust one part at a time without treating subjective changes as proof of a medical effect.
